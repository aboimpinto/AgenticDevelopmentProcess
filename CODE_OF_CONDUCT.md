# HEPHA Community Conduct

HEPHA aims to be a practical, respectful place for people with different
backgrounds and views on AI-assisted development.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Be specific, constructive, and willing to revise a claim.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and security boundaries.
- Credit the work and ideas of others.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest and material AI assistance.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, credentials, or unsafe instructions.
- Impersonation, deceptive provenance, or fabricated verification evidence.
- Repeated disruption after a maintainer has set a clear boundary.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and other spaces representing HEPHA.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the
[`aboimpinto` GitHub profile](https://github.com/aboimpinto). Do not include
sensitive details in a public message. The maintainer may edit or remove
content, limit participation, or block contributors when necessary to protect
the community and project.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where
appropriate, and made without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
